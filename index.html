About QuietPitch

QuietPitch is a spoiler-free football dashboard designed for fans who can't watch matches live. Check subtle 💡 Hints for context without ruining the score, or save matches to your personal archive.

Privacy & Data

No tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, and no ads. All your settings and bookmarks stay strictly local to your browser.
